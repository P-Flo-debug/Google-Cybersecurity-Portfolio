# Project 1: Ransomware Incident Documentation 🏥🛡️

## Project Overview
In this project, I acted as an Incident Handler for a healthcare clinic that suffered a devastating ransomware attack. The attack encrypted critical patient records (PII/SPII) and halted all business operations. My primary task was to perform initial documentation of the incident to facilitate a structured response and recovery.

## Technical Skills & Tools
* **Incident Documentation:** Using a standardized Incident Handler's Journal.
* **Forensic Analysis (5 W's):** Identifying the Who, What, When, Where, and Why of a security breach.
* **Social Engineering Identification:** Recognizing spear phishing as the initial access vector.
* **Administrative Controls:** Proposing security awareness training as a mitigation strategy.



## Incident Summary (The 5 W's)
Based on my investigation, the incident was broken down as follows:

* **Who:** An organized group of unethical hackers targeting the healthcare industry.
* **What:** A ransomware attack that encrypted medical records and demanded payment for a decryption key.
* **When:** Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m.
* **Where:** A small U.S. healthcare clinic specializing in primary care.
* **Why:** A staff member inadvertently downloaded a malicious attachment from a spear phishing email.

## Lessons Learned & Recommendations
This incident highlights a critical failure in human-layer security. Even without advanced technical tools at this stage, the following "Managerial Controls" are essential:
1. **Quarterly Security Awareness Training:** Specifically focused on identifying suspicious attachments and "hovering" over links.
2. **Reporting Culture:** Encouraging employees to report suspicious emails immediately to the IT/Security team.
