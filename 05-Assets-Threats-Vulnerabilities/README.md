# Course 5: Assets, Threats, and Vulnerabilities 🛡️
### Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate

This repository documents my work on the fifth course of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate. This module focuses on mastering **Risk Management**, **Asset Security**, and the **Attacker Mindset** through the application of industry-standard frameworks.

---

## 📁 Featured Projects

### 📑 [Project 1: Home Office Asset Inventory & Risk Classification](./project-1)
* **Goal:** Catalog hardware/software assets and classify them based on the **CIA Triad**.
* **Outcome:** Identified high-risk IoT assets and applied sensitivity levels to prevent lateral movement.

### 📑 [Project 2: Risk Register & Threat Analysis](./project-2)
* **Goal:** Perform a comprehensive risk assessment using a **Likelihood × Severity** scoring system.
* **Outcome:** Developed a roadmap for prioritizing security controls like MFA and database encryption.

### 📑 [Project 3: Data Privacy & Least Privilege Analysis](./project-3)
* **Goal:** Analyze a real-world breach to identify failures in the **Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP)**.
* **Outcome:** Applied **NIST SP 800-53 (AC-6)** standards to propose automated access revocation and RBAC.

### 📑 [Project 4: Decrypting Encrypted Messages (Linux/OpenSSL)](./project-4)
* **Goal:** Navigate the Linux file system to recover data protected by legacy and modern cryptography.
* **Outcome:** Used **OpenSSL** and **Bash** logic to reverse a Caesar cipher and decrypt AES-256 files.
